    📸 Posture Guide

    Use 🧍 or 🤸 to indicate proper posture for exercises.

    🦿 Lunges

  • ✅ Step forward with one leg, keeping your back straight and both knees at 90°. Avoid leaning forward.

    • 🏋️‍♂️ Deadlifts

  • ✅ Keep your back neutral, lift with your legs, not your spine. Bar close to your shins.

    • 🛌 Crunches

  • ✅ Keep your hands lightly behind your head. Engage your core and don’t pull your neck.

    • 💪 Bicep Curls

  • ✅ Elbows tucked in, don’t swing weights. Controlled motion both up and down.

    • ⛰️ Mountain Climbers

  • ✅ Keep your back straight and core tight. Drive your knees toward your chest quickly and steadily.
